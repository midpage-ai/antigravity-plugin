# Midpage Legal Research

Research US case law, statutes, and regulations, plus live dockets from federal and state courts, from Antigravity.

The plugin connects Antigravity to the Midpage MCP server at `https://app.midpage.ai/mcp`. Use it to find court opinions by issue, citation, or keyword, read and quote from them, review federal and state law, and pull PACER and state court dockets and filings.

A [Midpage](https://www.midpage.ai) account is required.

## Sign in

After installing, sign in to Midpage in your browser:

- In the Antigravity app, open **Customizations → Installed** and click **Authenticate** next to Midpage, then paste the code back into the app.
- In the Antigravity CLI, open `/plugin`, expand Midpage under **Installed**, and choose **Sign in**.
